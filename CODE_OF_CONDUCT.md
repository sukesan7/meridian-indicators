# Community code of conduct

Meridian welcomes technical disagreement, reproducible criticism and careful research. Participation must remain respectful and useful.

Expected behavior includes discussing ideas rather than attacking people, documenting claims, respecting privacy, accepting correction and avoiding promotional spam or deceptive performance claims.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential sharing, market manipulation coordination and knowingly fabricated results are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove disruptive contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project.
